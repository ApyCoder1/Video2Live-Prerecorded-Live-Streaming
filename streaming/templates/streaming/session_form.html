VPS Streaming

How the streaming pipeline works.

Your VPS runs the streaming process.
Videos are loaded from the selected playlist.
FFmpeg processes the selected video and sends the stream to the configured RTMP destination.
The session mode determines whether the playlist runs once or repeats.
Scheduled sessions can be managed through Celery.
Make sure the RTMP destination and stream key are correct and your VPS has FFmpeg installed before starting the session.

Never expose your YouTube stream key in HTML, JavaScript, logs, API responses, or browser data attributes.
